Research

Uncertainty-aware historical cause-of-death coding with fine-tuned language models Grade 12

Bachelor thesis, DTU · 2026

I fine-tuned language models to give 19th-century, multilingual death records a standard cause-of-death code, out of more than 14,000 possible labels. The models estimate how sure they are, so the uncertain cases go to a human instead of being guessed. I presented the work at a conference in May 2026, it got the top grade, and a journal paper is in preparation.

Adversarial attacks for image protection

DTU project

Can adversarial noise protect artists' images from being scraped for AI training? We compared three attacks (FGSM, PGD and Carlini-Wagner) on image classifiers: how much they hurt accuracy for a given amount of noise, how they change the model's confidence, and whether the noise still works on a model it wasn't made for.
